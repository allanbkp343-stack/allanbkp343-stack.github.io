NEMESIS — Autonomous Purple Team Platform

Multi-agent orchestration across the full assessment lifecycle: reconnaissance, exploitation, escalation, and reporting — under human checkpoints, with evidence that survives scrutiny.

Most of what limits security work isn't a lack of tools. It's fragmentation: findings scattered across terminals, context that dies between sessions, evidence that lives nowhere, and the same dead ends re-explored on every engagement. NEMESIS was built to fix that — not by adding more output, but by adding memory, discipline, and proof.

The goal was never more output. It was evidence that holds up — and work that compounds.

NEMESIS stays private; its lab results, methodology, and lessons show up across this site — sanitized and redacted. Details available on request.
