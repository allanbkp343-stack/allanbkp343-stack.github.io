01 — Selected Work

A platform that runs assessments the way I would.

Margin noteBranch decisions are scored on evidence strength, knowledge-base support, operational noise, and complexity — and recorded, so the reasoning survives the session.

Most of what limits security work isn't a lack of tools. It's fragmentation: findings scattered across terminals, context that dies between sessions, evidence that lives nowhere, and the same dead ends re-explored on every engagement.

I'm building a private platform that addresses that directly. It orchestrates specialist agents across the full assessment lifecycle — reconnaissance through reporting — under a model I actually trust: every action logged, every high-risk step approved, every failed path recorded so it is never retried.

How it works

Margin noteA compounding knowledge base gives each new session a head start — techniques, failures, and context persist across engagements.

Specialist agents (recon, web, identity, escalation, post-exploitation) work under a decision engine that reasons in branches: it weighs alternatives before acting, and backtracks deliberately instead of retrying. State is written continuously, so a crash or a context reset never costs the engagement its memory.

Guardrails matter more than autonomy

Margin noteIn enterprise modes, Ring 3+ actions — exploitation, C2, destructive operations — wait for an explicit human decision before they run.

Every capability is classified by risk before it is allowed to exist. High-risk actions pause for explicit operator approval; scope and phase gates are enforced at the tool layer, not by good intentions. Failed attempts are treated as first-class results — recorded, searchable, and never repeated. That discipline is the difference between an autonomous system you can deploy and one you merely demo.

The goal was never more output. It was evidence that holds up — and work that compounds.

The platform is private R&D. Its lab results, methodology, and lessons show up in the writing below, sanitized and redacted. Details available on request.

04 — Method

How the work gets done.

1

Scope

Agree objectives, constraints, and evidence handling before anything touches a target.

2

Recon

Map services, versions, and the stories the target tells — patiently, because everything else depends on this.

3

Model

Turn observations into hypotheses worth testing: trust boundaries, weak seams, paths that would actually matter.

4

Validate

Test controlled steps and record what fails as carefully as what works. No finding without reproduction.

5

Escalate

Follow the chain with the minimum action necessary — foothold to privilege, user to domain, host to network.

6

Evidence

Preserve provenance: commands, outputs, timestamps, and the decision trail behind every step.

7

Report

Translate findings into impact, root cause, detection opportunities, and remediation that gets acted on.

8

Retest

Verify the fixes. The loop isn't closed until someone checks — and records what remains.
